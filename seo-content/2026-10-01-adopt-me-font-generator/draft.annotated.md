## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an **Adopt Me font generator**?

It's a tool that turns normal letters into look-alike **Unicode** characters, like bubble, small caps, or cursive letters, that you can paste into Adopt Me. The styled text is still regular characters, so it works without downloading a font.

### How do I put a fancy font on my Adopt Me pet's name?

Copy a style from this page, then equip your pet in Adopt Me, tap it, and tap the **pencil icon** next to its name. Paste the styled name and confirm. **Renaming is free**, so you can try a few styles.

### Why does my pet's name show as **####**?

**Roblox's text filter** replaced it. Adopt Me runs on Roblox, so every pet name and sign is checked, and anything the filter blocks or can't read becomes **hash marks**. Try a shorter name, a plainer style like bubble or small caps, or style one word only.

### Which fonts work best for Adopt Me **pet names**?

**Bubble**, small caps, and bold are the most reliable. They're easy for the filter to read and show on almost every device. Cursive and double-struck often work too, and simple symbols like ♡, ★, and ✦ are usually kept.

### Is there a character limit for Adopt Me **pet names**?

Yes, but Adopt Me doesn't publish the exact number, and fan guides disagree. Keep pet names short. Bubble and small caps letters count as one character each, while bold and cursive letters can count as two, so they fill the limit faster.

### Why does my friend see my pet's name as **boxes** or question marks?

Their device doesn't have a **glyph** for that character. This happens most on older phones and tablets. Bubble and bold have the widest support, so switch to one of those if friends can't read the name.

### Does a fancy pet name make my pet worth more in trades?

No. A pet's **trade value** comes from its type, rarity, and whether it's **Neon** or **Mega Neon**. A styled name only changes how it looks, so don't trade more for a pet because of its name.

### Can I use fancy fonts to get around the Roblox filter?

No, and you shouldn't try. **Roblox's filter** checks styled letters too, and using symbols to hide blocked words breaks Roblox's **Community Standards**. It can get your text removed or your account moderated.

### What font is the Adopt Me logo?

The Adopt Me logo is a designed **wordmark**, not a font you can type, so no generator can paste it as text. For a similar rounded, playful look in a name, bubble letters are the closest copy-paste style.

### Do Adopt Me fonts work on mobile, Xbox, and PlayStation?

Usually, yes. Adopt Me runs on every device Roblox supports, and the fonts are standard **Unicode**. A few rare styles may show as **boxes** on some devices, so check how a name looks on the device you play on most.
