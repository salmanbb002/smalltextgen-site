# Text Formatting Cheat Sheet: Bold, Italic, Underline & Strikethrough for Every Platform

The shortcut or symbol for **bold**, **italic**, **underline**, and **strikethrough** in every major app, and the copy-paste fallback for apps that have none.

Every app formats text differently. **WhatsApp** uses *asterisks* for **bold**, **Discord** uses **two**, **Google Docs** uses Ctrl+B, and Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn posts have no formatting at all. This cheat sheet lists the native method for bold, **italic**, **underline**, and **strikethrough** in each app, and the **Unicode** copy-paste fallback for apps that don't support them.

The rule of thumb: use an app's own formatting when it has it, because it stays searchable and accessible. Use copy-paste **Unicode** styles from the [bold](/tools/bold), [italic](/tools/italic), [underline](/tools/underline), and [strikethrough](/tools/strikethrough) generators only where an app has no option.

## How do you format text in messaging apps?

Messaging apps use symbols around the words. Type the symbol, the text, and the same symbol again, with no spaces between the symbol and the word, and the app formats it when you send. The catch is that each app picked slightly different symbols.

### WhatsApp formatting

**WhatsApp** uses single symbols: *bold*, _italic_, ~**strikethrough**~, and ```monospace``` with three backticks. Since 2024 it also supports `inline code`, bulleted lists that start with - or *, numbered lists, and > quotes. You can combine styles, like *_bold italic_*, but WhatsApp has no **underline**. On WhatsApp Desktop, Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, and Ctrl+Shift+X also work. These codes only work in chats, so for your profile name, About, or group name, paste [Unicode bold](/tools/bold) instead.

### Discord formatting

**Discord** uses **Markdown**: **bold**, *italic*, __underline__, ~~**strikethrough**~~, ||spoiler||, and `code`. You can stack them, like ***__bold **italic** underline__***. At the start of a line, # makes a header (up to ###), -# makes small subtext, and > makes a quote. None of this works in display names, nicknames, or statuses, which need **Unicode** styles. See [tiny text on Discord](/guides/tiny-text-discord) for the subtext trick.

### Telegram and Slack formatting

**Telegram** takes **bold**, __italic__, ~~**strikethrough**~~, and ||spoiler|| on desktop and web. On every device, you can select text and pick a style, including **underline**, from the menu. **Slack** is closer to **WhatsApp**: *bold*, _italic_, ~strikethrough~, and `code`, plus Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, and Ctrl+Shift+X. Slack has no underline.

## How do you format text in Google Docs and Word?

Document editors use keyboard shortcuts. Select the text first, then press the shortcut. **Bold**, **italic**, and **underline** are the same almost everywhere: Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, and Ctrl+U on Windows, or ⌘+B, ⌘+I, and ⌘+U on a Mac.

**Strikethrough** is the one that differs. In **Google Docs** and Sheets it's Alt+Shift+5 on Windows or ⌘+Shift+X on a Mac. Word for Windows has no default strikethrough shortcut, so press Ctrl+D and tick Strikethrough, or click the abc button on the Home tab. Word for Mac uses ⌘+Shift+X, and Excel uses Ctrl+5. Word also has a double **underline** (Ctrl+Shift+D) and a double strikethrough in the Font box.

## How do you format text on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X?

You can't, natively. Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and LinkedIn posts have no **bold**, **italic**, **underline**, or **strikethrough** options, and X only offers some formatting to Premium subscribers on the web. The workaround is **Unicode**: special characters that already look bold, italic, underlined, or crossed out, which you copy from a generator and paste in.

Use the [bold text generator](/tools/bold) for headers and hooks, the [italic text generator](/tools/italic) for emphasis, the [underline text generator](/tools/underline) or [strikethrough text generator](/tools/strikethrough) for lines, and the [cursive font generator](/tools/cursive) for a handwritten look. Instagram is covered in detail in the [Instagram bio fonts guide](/guides/small-text-instagram-bio), and X in [fonts for X/Twitter](/guides/fonts-for-twitter-x).

## Native formatting vs Unicode text: which should you use?

Use native formatting whenever the app has it. It formats normal letters, so the text stays searchable, screen readers read it normally, and it counts as normal characters. It's lost when you copy it into a plain-text field, though.

Use **Unicode** only where there's no native option. It works anywhere text goes, but each styled letter is a different character. That means hashtags and search may not match it, screen readers may read it letter by letter, and **bold** or **italic** letters can count as two characters toward a limit. **Underline** and **strikethrough** also get stripped by some apps. Keep Unicode to short accents like a name, a header, or one key phrase.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I make text bold in WhatsApp?

Put an asterisk on each side of the words, like *this*, with no spaces next to the asterisks. **WhatsApp** shows it **bold** when you send. On WhatsApp Desktop you can also select the text and press Ctrl+B.

### How do I underline text in Discord?

Wrap the text in two underscores on each side, like __this__. You can combine it with **bold** or **italic**, like **__bold underline__**.

### Can you underline text in WhatsApp or Slack?

No. Neither app has **underline** formatting. If you need an underline, paste text from the underline text generator, which adds a combining line under each letter, though some apps strip it.

### What's the difference between WhatsApp and Discord formatting?

**WhatsApp** uses single symbols (*bold*, _italic_, ~strike~), while **Discord** uses **Markdown** with doubled symbols for **bold** and **strikethrough** (**bold**, ~~strike~~). Discord also supports __underline__ and ||spoilers||, which WhatsApp doesn't.

### What is the strikethrough shortcut?

It depends on the app: Alt+Shift+5 in **Google Docs** on Windows, ⌘+Shift+X in most Mac apps, Ctrl+5 in Excel, and Ctrl+Shift+X in **Slack** and **Telegram**. Word for Windows has no default shortcut, so use Ctrl+D and tick **Strikethrough**.

### How do I bold text on Instagram or LinkedIn?

Neither has a **bold** option in bios, captions, or regular posts. Make the bold text with a **Unicode** bold generator, copy it, and paste it in. LinkedIn articles and newsletters do have a real bold button.

### Does Markdown work everywhere?

No. **Discord**, Reddit, GitHub, Notion, and **Telegram** desktop support **Markdown**, but **WhatsApp** and **Slack** use their own single-symbol versions, and Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn posts ignore it completely.

### Why didn't my formatting work?

Usually there's a space between the symbol and the word, like * **bold** *, or the symbols don't match on both sides. Also check that you're in a message field: formatting codes don't work in names, bios, or statuses.

### Is Unicode bold or italic text bad for accessibility?

It can be. Screen readers may read **Unicode**-styled letters one at a time by their technical names, or skip them. Use native formatting where it exists, and keep important information in plain text.

### How do I remove formatting from pasted text?

Paste as plain text with Ctrl+Shift+V (⌘+Shift+Option+V in many Mac apps) to drop native formatting. **Unicode** styles are characters, not formatting, so they stay until you retype the text or convert it back to normal letters.
